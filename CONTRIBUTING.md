# Contributing to Skaphos

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Skaphos, a project of
**Rillan AI LLC**, a Missouri limited liability company doing business as
Skaphos.

This is the organization-wide contribution policy and applies to every
repository in the [Skaphos organization](https://github.com/skaphos). Individual
repositories may add their own build, test, and style guidance; where a
repository ships its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, follow it **in addition to** the
certification and licensing requirements below.

## Certifying and licensing your contribution

All contributions to Skaphos repositories require **both** of the following:

### 1. DCO sign-off (every commit)

Every commit must be signed off under the
[Developer Certificate of Origin v1.1](https://developercertificate.org/):

    git commit -s

The `Signed-off-by:` line certifies that you have the right to submit the
work under the project's open-source license.

### 2. Contributor License Agreement (once)

Before your first pull request can be merged, you (or your employer, if you
are contributing on its behalf) must sign the Skaphos Contributor License
Agreement with Rillan AI LLC:

- [Individual CLA](legal/skaphos-individual-cla.md)
- [Entity CLA](legal/skaphos-entity-cla.md) — for contributions owned by your employer

The CLA is a license, not a copyright assignment: you keep ownership of your
contribution and grant Rillan AI LLC and downstream recipients a broad,
irrevocable license to use it. This keeps long-term stewardship of the
project (including any future relicensing decisions) with a single legal
entity while your rights in your own work are preserved.

Questions about contribution licensing may be directed to legal@skaphos.io.
